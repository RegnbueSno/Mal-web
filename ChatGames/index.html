⌬ Function Summary

 The Pokémon Community Game (PCG) bot is a third-party subsystem linked to Mal's environment through external network protocols. It enables users to capture, train, and trade data-creatures known as Pokémon — fragments of a wider universe that Mal allows to exist within controlled boundaries.

PCG functions as a persistent collection framework, separate from Mal's internal architecture but monitored through his observation layer. Each action — catch, duel, trade — is executed on the Pokémon Community servers, while Mal logs and translates outputs for local display.
