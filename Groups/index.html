For The Vibes

This is a streaming team!
We aim to create a positive and inclusive space for both streamers and hero shooter enthusiasts, fostering a community where everyone can feel welcome and supported.

In this group we have a discord for creators to collaborate and plan events together!
If you wish to apply to join the discord wether you are a hero shooter enthusiast or a streamer who sometimes play hero shooters, you can apply here -> For The Vibes Applications and Rules.
there are two application forms, one for streamers and one for hero shooter enthusiasts, so make sure to fill out the correct one!

We hope to see many loving and passionate people join our community, and we can't wait to create amazing content together!

With loving regard.

- For The Vibes Team
