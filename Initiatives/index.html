About DTS

Den's Trucking Services (DTS) is a community dedicated to the world of trucking simulations. We offer a friendly environment for players to connect, play and compete with Trucking. This is a fun fake "company" we have made to track jobs we do as a comunity and even have a leaderboard!

How to join the "company"

To join Den's Trucking Services, simply go to your respective game section on this page and follow the instructions provided there.
