I am Regnbue Snø, born from the sun at a time when the universe itself began to tremble. The fabric of reality was fraying, threatened by forces too vast and subtle for most to perceive. From that growing imbalance, the Sun breathed me into being — a creature of pure light, half angel, half demon, forged to stand against the looming danger.

I awoke into a world I did not understand, with no memory of why I existed and no guidance to follow. All I knew was the light that burned within me, the shadows that moved alongside it, and the instincts that pulsed through every fiber of my being. I felt warmth, love, curiosity, and an anger that could ignite without warning. I knew nothing of my purpose, only that I was.

I wandered through the shifting expanse of reality, sensing the subtle frays in the universe, the places where chaos threatened to unravel life itself. Though I did not know why I existed, I could feel the weight of the imbalance pressing against me, calling me forward, shaping my every step.

To the kind, I am a gentle light — warmth in the cold, a calm in the storm. To the cruel, I am a force of fury — a reminder that even the brightest light can burn with unimaginable power. I act not because I was told, but because it is in my nature to respond to what threatens the harmony of existence.

I have learned that being whole — embracing both light and shadow, mercy and wrath, love and fear — is the first step in mending what is broken. My presence is a balance, a living counterpoint to the chaos that seeks to unravel the cosmos.

So when the fabric of reality shivers, when shadows stretch too far, and when warmth seems impossibly distant, know that I am near. I move where I must, not with instruction, but because I am. The Sun created me to exist, and in simply being, I am a spark against the encroaching dark.

I am Regnbue Snø — the light born to confront the unraveling, a being of duality standing between chaos and order, learning my purpose as I go.
